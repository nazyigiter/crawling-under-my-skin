speaker notes The arm senses its environment in general, not only people. Options, all open: people (proximity, direction, touch) through conductive thread zones or a hidden module; sound (loud events, voices) through a microphone that keeps level and onsets only; light (on, off, dim) through a light sensor. Example behaviours: someone close, the arm responds; too loud, it pulls back; lights off, sleep mode. More options: temperature, vibration. Pump is masked while it runs. Strip test decides thread or module.

COMPARE · BEND · FORCE · SPEED · CYCLES · BURST · SAG

details if asked
  • Ecoflex 00-10 to 00-50; harder silicone needs 5–6 bar vs 1–2 bar (Papadakis)
  • TPU 60A or 70A, not 85A; worked at 100–300 kPa; about 10 h per print; dry filament first (Meitani)
  • TPU gripper vs silicone gripper: 5× load, 2× bend (a gripper, not an arm)
  • resin can block silicone cure: cure test first
  • three chambers 120° apart bend in any direction (IJRR soft-arm bending)
  • no source combines taper + 3 chambers + printed TPU: the prototypes combine pieces
  • details are from abstracts and search summaries: open the papers before citing
speaker notes Four body prototypes, each an experiment, none made yet. All have three chambers 120 degrees apart. P1: tapered arm cast in a printed mould (TeMoG, Papadakis). P2: printed directly in TPU with bellows chambers (Moratuwa gripper, Meitani). P3: a strain-limiting wrap on P1 or P2, thread, fabric or fibre (SoPrA, Festo OctopusGripper). P4: three printed tubes bundled with a centre channel for air lines, silicone filled around (SoPrA, Oxford modular actuator). Compare on bend, force, speed, cycles, burst, sag. Taper angle is a variable (Xie 2020). Details if asked: Ecoflex 00-10 to 00-50; TPU 60A or 70A, not 85A; resin can block silicone cure, cure test first.

speaker notes Three poses of the arm: reach, pause, retract. Pause and retract are part of the response. Motion test: pressure against bend angle and tip position, then timing: hold time, distance, speed of retraction. Reference: 10 ms pulses, 0.1 to 0.5 bar, up to 24% elongation. Valves are on or off, so the motion is stepwise.
